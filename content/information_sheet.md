## Study title
Human–Multi-Agent Teaming for Secure Software Development

## What is this study about?
AI coding assistants can help people write code quickly, but the code they suggest is not always secure.
This study compares two ways of working with AI while writing code: with a **team of specialist AI agents**
(a planner, a threat modeller, a code reviewer, a test writer and a facilitator), and with a **single AI assistant**.

## What will I do?
You will complete two short programming tasks in a code editor in this browser: one with the agent team and one
with the single assistant. Each part has a time limit (about 25 minutes, CONFIRM). Afterwards you will answer a short
questionnaire about your experience. The whole session takes about one hour (CONFIRM).

## What information is collected?
- The code you write, your chat messages with the AI, which AI suggestions you approve or reject, and test results.
- Your questionnaire answers.

Everything is stored under a participant ID such as P07. **No name, email, student number or other identifying
information is collected.**

## External AI service
Your code and chat messages are sent to an external AI service to generate the AI's responses.
Please do not type any personal information.

## Do I have to take part?
No. Taking part is voluntary, and you can stop at any time without giving a reason.
